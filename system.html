Design system · v1

Knots as primitives.

Every line, icon and ornament on the site is built from six things found in the physical work: the knots, the cord ends, and the brass rod each piece hangs from. The digital presence carries the same elements a guest would see beside the work on a wall.

Six primitives

Drawn with a single 1.15 px cord stroke, rounded ends, in ink, reed or bone. Brass is reserved for hardware.

The organising idea

The page hangs
like a piece.

Every Knotty Things work hangs from a brass rod, its body is joined knot by knot, and it ends in a fringe. The site follows the same anatomy.

The header is the rod, edged with lark's heads once you scroll. Sections are the body, separated by square knots. The footer ends in a fringe of cords. Page changes tie a square knot in the dark.

Rules

How to keep it quiet.

One knot per view

A primitive marks a structural moment: a section change, a list start, the active page. Never repeat it as decoration.

Full-bleed only to open

Only a page's opening image runs edge to edge. Every other image sits framed in the grid at 4:5 or 3:4, on the bone ground.

Brass is hardware

Brass appears only where metal would be in the studio: rods, end caps, the rod-edge of the header.

Still by default

Motion is limited to a slow settle, a knot tying during page changes, and cords swaying when sound is on. Nothing loops for attention.

Cord weight, not border

Lines are drawn as cord: 1–1.25 px, rounded ends, in reed or ink at low opacity. No boxes, no shadows, no rounded cards.

From the work, to the room

Use the same primitives on hang tags, packaging cards, lookbooks and studio signage so the site and the physical presence feel like one atelier.
