Privacy policy · Last updated 10 November 2026

Your details, handled with care.

This page explains what Knotty Things collects through theknottythings.com, why, and what you can ask us to do with it.

Who we are

Knotty Things is a fiber art studio in Hyderabad, India. For anything about this policy or your details, write to contact@theknottythings.com or call +91 97170 40077.

What we collect

We only collect what you choose to send us through the commission brief, the trade request and the contact form:

  • your name, email address and, if you add them, your phone number, company and city;
  • details of your project, such as the space, size, timeline, the work you are interested in and any message you write;
  • the date and time you sent it and the page you sent it from.

The site does not use accounts, cookies, advertising trackers or analytics, and it does not store anything in your browser.

Why we use it

To reply to you, understand your project, prepare a concept or proposal, and follow up on that conversation. We do not sell your details, and we do not add you to a mailing list without asking.

Where it is kept

Your enquiry is emailed to our studio inbox and recorded in a private spreadsheet, both in our Google Workspace account. Only the Knotty Things team can access them. If we later move our records to a customer-relationship system, your details will move with the same care.

Services this site relies on

  • GitHub Pages hosts the website. Like any web host, it may briefly log technical details such as your IP address to keep the service secure.
  • Google Fonts provides the typefaces, so your browser contacts Google when a page loads.
  • Google Workspace receives and stores the forms you send.

Links to Instagram take you to Instagram, where its own privacy policy applies.

How long we keep it

For as long as we need it to respond to you and look after the project or relationship that follows, or until you ask us to delete it.

Your choices

You can ask us at any time to show you the details we hold about you, correct them, or delete them, and you can withdraw your consent to us contacting you. Write to contact@theknottythings.com and we will respond promptly.

Children

This site is meant for adults planning artwork for homes and businesses. We do not knowingly collect details from anyone under 18.

Changes

If we change how we handle your details, we will update this page and the date at the top.
