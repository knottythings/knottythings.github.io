Sculpture · Installation · 2026

From the wall, into space.

In 2026 the studio set macramé on stainless steel and lifted it off the wall: sculpture that occupies a room the way water does, and installations that hang across a ceiling.

What it takes to
build at this scale.

Every sculptural commission moves through the same disciplines, from the first sketch to the final suspension.

01

Form generation

Sketched on paper, refined on screen, tested in 3D before a single cord is cut.

02

Structure

Stainless steel and brass frames, made with local vendors to a vision they have never seen before.

03

The knot

Hundreds of hours by master craftsmen. Prototypes are refined, and sometimes cut in two.

04

Installation

Delivered and suspended on site. Hands at work, art in progress.
